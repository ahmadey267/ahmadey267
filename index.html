Questions

Before you book.

Do you operate at night and at weekends?
Yes. Our transfer and concierge services run around the clock, every day, so late night and early morning flight schedules are covered as standard rather than as an exception.
What happens if my flight is delayed?
We track flights in real time and adjust pickup times for delays and early arrivals, so your driver is there when you actually land rather than when you were scheduled to.
Can you handle groups and delegations?
Yes. We coordinate group airport and hotel transfers, conference movement, corporate shuttles and group safari bookings, keeping the whole party on one schedule.
Do you arrange accommodation as well as transport?
Yes. We book luxury, boutique, mid range and corporate accommodation at negotiated rates, and we align check in times with your transfers so nobody waits in a lobby.
Are your vehicles insured and your drivers vetted?
All vehicles are fully insured and well maintained, and every driver is trained and vetted. Safety and security are treated as the baseline, not as an upgrade.
Do you work with corporate clients on contract?
Yes. We offer flexible long term transport contracts with dedicated vehicles, uniformed drivers, executive discretion and consolidated billing your finance team can reconcile.

Tell us the dates and the standard you expect.

A single transfer, a week of executive movement or a full safari itinerary. We will come back with a plan and honest pricing.
