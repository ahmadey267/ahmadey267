About Haviy Global Services

A Nairobi based travel and transportation solutions provider, built to deliver African hospitality at international service standards.

Company overview

Premier travel and transportation, delivered end to end

Haviy Global Services is a premier travel and transportation solutions provider dedicated to delivering seamless, safe and luxurious experiences for both individual and corporate clients.

Headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, Haviy Global Services specialises in airport transfers, hotel transfers, corporate transport, VIP services, hotel and safari bookings, city tours, airport meet and assist, and bespoke concierge solutions.

Our goal is to redefine excellence in travel services by combining local expertise with international standards of professionalism, safety and personalised care.

Our core objectives

What we are working towards

  • To provide world class transport and travel services that meet international standards.
  • To become the preferred transport and concierge partner for corporate clients.
  • To enhance tourism experiences for international visitors through curated tours.
  • To ensure seamless airport experiences through meet and assist services.
  • To deliver luxury and VIP services with professionalism and discretion.

Why choose us

Five reasons clients stay with HGS

  • One stop travel and concierge solution

    We provide transport, travel, hospitality, concierge and photography services under one trusted brand. This eliminates the need to deal with multiple service providers and ensures seamless coordination.

  • Professional and experienced team

    Our team consists of trained drivers, tour guides, concierge personnel and support staff with extensive industry experience. Every client is served with professionalism, courtesy and attention to detail.

  • Punctuality and reliability

    We understand that time is critical in travel and business. Our services are designed to ensure on time pickups, smooth transitions and dependable service delivery.

  • Personalised client experience

    Every client's needs are unique, and we tailor our services accordingly. From customised itineraries to special requests, we deliver solutions that match individual preferences.

  • Safety, security and comfort

    All our vehicles are well maintained, insured and operated by vetted professionals. Client safety, comfort and peace of mind remain our top priorities.
