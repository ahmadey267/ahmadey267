About the company

A Nairobi based travel and transportation solutions provider, built to deliver African hospitality at international service standards.

Company overview

Premier travel and transport, delivered end to end.

Haviy Global Services is a premier travel and transportation solutions provider dedicated to delivering seamless, safe and luxurious experiences for both individual and corporate clients.

Headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, Haviy Global Services specialises in airport transfers, hotel transfers, corporate transport, VIP services, hotel and safari bookings, city tours, airport meet and assist, and bespoke concierge solutions.

Our goal is to redefine excellence in travel services by combining local expertise with international standards of professionalism, safety and personalised care.
